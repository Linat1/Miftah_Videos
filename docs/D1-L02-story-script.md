# يَوْمُ الأَرْبِعَاءِ · Yusuf's Busy Wednesday

**D1-L02 · Telling the Time** · Miftah Arabic · story episode script

**Cast:** Yusuf (a student, always late) · Maryam (his big sister) · Aisha (his classmate) · Noor (the teacher) · the waiter · a narrator (English, for the tips)

**Running time:** about 2:44 (it adjusts to your recordings)

---

### Title card

*<span dir="rtl">يَوْمُ الأَرْبِعَاءِ</span>: Yusuf's Busy Wednesday*

## Scene 1 · Bedroom, 7:30 a.m. <span dir="rtl">(٧:٣٠ صَبَاحًا)</span>

*An alarm clock rings. On stage: Yusuf. The wall clock shows 7:30 a.m.*

*(Maryam rushes in from the right. Yusuf is still half asleep.)*

**MARYAM** *(calling loudly from the doorway)* · `s1-01`

> <span dir="rtl">يُوسُفُ! اِسْتَيْقِظْ!</span>
>
> *Yusuf! Wake up!*

*(Yusuf is confused.)*

**YUSUF** *(sleepy, yawning)* · `s1-02`

> <span dir="rtl">كَمِ السَّاعَةُ؟</span>
>
> *What time is it?*

**MARYAM** *(matter-of-fact)* · `s1-03`

> <span dir="rtl">السَّاعَةُ السَّابِعَةُ وَالنِّصْفُ.</span>
>
> *It's half past seven.*

**MARYAM** *(urgent)* · `s1-04`

> <span dir="rtl">يَبْدَأُ الدَّوَامُ فِي الثَّامِنَةِ إِلَّا عَشْرَ دَقَائِقَ!</span>
>
> *School starts at ten to eight!*

*(Yusuf is shocked.)*

**YUSUF** *(panicking)* · `s1-05`

> <span dir="rtl">مَاذَا؟! عِشْرُونَ دَقِيقَةً فَقَطْ!</span>
>
> *What?! Only twenty minutes!*

*(Yusuf dashes off to the left.)*

### 💡 Miftah tip

*3D clock cut-away. On screen: Minutes past: keep the hour + وَ.*

**NARRATOR** *(clear, friendly narrator)* · `tip-1`

> Up to half past, keep the same hour and add wa.

## Scene 2 · School corridor, 7:45 a.m. <span dir="rtl">(٧:٤٥ صَبَاحًا)</span>

*On stage: Aisha. The wall clock shows 7:45 a.m.*

*(Yusuf rushes in from the left. Yusuf is flustered.)*

**AISHA** *(cheerful)* · `s2-01`

> <span dir="rtl">صَبَاحَ الخَيْرِ يَا يُوسُفُ!</span>
>
> *Good morning, Yusuf!*

**YUSUF** *(out of breath)* · `s2-02`

> <span dir="rtl">صَبَاحَ النُّورِ! كَمِ السَّاعَةُ الآنَ؟</span>
>
> *Good morning! What time is it now?*

**AISHA** *(pointing, amused)* · `s2-03`

> <span dir="rtl">اُنْظُرْ إِلَى السَّاعَةِ!</span>
>
> *Look at the clock!*

**YUSUF** *(slowly, reading the clock (wrongly!))* · `s2-04`

> <span dir="rtl">السَّاعَةُ السَّابِعَةُ إِلَّا الرُّبْعَ...</span>
>
> *It's quarter to seven...*

*(Yusuf does a happy little jump.)*

**YUSUF** *(relieved, relaxed)* · `s2-05`

> <span dir="rtl">عِنْدِي وَقْتٌ كَثِيرٌ!</span>
>
> *I've got lots of time!*

*(Aisha is shocked.)*

**AISHA** *(laughing, correcting him)* · `s2-06`

> <span dir="rtl">لَا، لَا! السَّاعَةُ الثَّامِنَةُ إِلَّا الرُّبْعَ!</span>
>
> *No, no! It's quarter to EIGHT!*

*(Yusuf can't believe it.)*

**YUSUF** *(horrified)* · `s2-07`

> <span dir="rtl">خَمْسُ دَقَائِقَ فَقَطْ؟!</span>
>
> *Only five minutes?!*

*(Yusuf dashes off to the right.)*

### 💡 Miftah tip

*3D clock cut-away. On screen: Minutes to: the NEXT hour + إِلَّا.*

**NARRATOR** *(clear, friendly narrator)* · `tip-2`

> After half past, name the next hour, then illaa.

## Scene 3 · Classroom, 8:00 a.m. <span dir="rtl">(٨:٠٠ صَبَاحًا)</span>

*On stage: Noor. The wall clock shows 8:00 a.m.*

**NOOR** *(warm, teacherly)* · `s3-01`

> <span dir="rtl">صَبَاحَ الخَيْرِ! يَبْدَأُ الدَّرْسُ الآنَ.</span>
>
> *Good morning! The lesson begins now.*

*(Yusuf rushes in from the right.)*

*(Yusuf is flustered.)*

**YUSUF** *(still out of breath)* · `s3-02`

> <span dir="rtl">مَتَى يَنْتَهِي الدَّرْسُ؟</span>
>
> *When does the lesson end?*

**NOOR** *(clear, pointing to the board)* · `s3-03`

> <span dir="rtl">يَنْتَهِي فِي التَّاسِعَةِ إِلَّا الرُّبْعَ.</span>
>
> *It ends at quarter to nine.*

**YUSUF** *(hopeful)* · `s3-04`

> <span dir="rtl">وَمَتَى تَبْدَأُ الاِسْتِرَاحَةُ؟</span>
>
> *And when does the break begin?*

**NOOR** *(kind but firm)* · `s3-05`

> <span dir="rtl">بَعْدَ الدَّرْسِ، وَتَسْتَمِرُّ عِشْرِينَ دَقِيقَةً.</span>
>
> *After the lesson. It lasts twenty minutes.*

*(Yusuf can't believe it.)*

**YUSUF** *(disappointed)* · `s3-06`

> <span dir="rtl">عِشْرُونَ دَقِيقَةً فَقَطْ؟!</span>
>
> *Only twenty minutes?!*

*(Noor is flustered.)*

### 💡 Miftah tip

*3D clock cut-away. On screen: يَبْدَأُ begins · يَنْتَهِي ends · يَسْتَمِرُّ lasts.*

**NARRATOR** *(clear, friendly narrator)* · `tip-3`

> Begins, ends, lasts: yabda'u, yantahee, yastamirru.

## Scene 4 · Café, 5:55 p.m. <span dir="rtl">(٥:٥٥ مَسَاءً)</span>

*On stage: Aisha, Yusuf, Waiter. The wall clock shows 5:55 p.m.*

**WAITER** *(friendly, polite)* · `s4-01`

> <span dir="rtl">تَفَضَّلَا!</span>
>
> *Here you are!*

**AISHA** *(curious)* · `s4-02`

> <span dir="rtl">مَتَى يَبْدَأُ التَّمْرِينُ؟</span>
>
> *When does training begin?*

**YUSUF** *(proud, confident)* · `s4-03`

> <span dir="rtl">يَبْدَأُ فِي السَّادِسَةِ إِلَّا الرُّبْعَ وَيَسْتَمِرُّ مُدَّةَ سَاعَةٍ وَالنِّصْفِ.</span>
>
> *It begins at quarter to six and lasts an hour and a half.*

**AISHA** *(casual)* · `s4-04`

> <span dir="rtl">كَمِ السَّاعَةُ الآنَ؟</span>
>
> *What time is it now?*

**WAITER** *(helpful)* · `s4-05`

> <span dir="rtl">السَّاعَةُ السَّادِسَةُ إِلَّا خَمْسَ دَقَائِقَ.</span>
>
> *It's five to six.*

*(Yusuf is shocked.)*

**YUSUF** *(shocked)* · `s4-06`

> <span dir="rtl">مَاذَا؟!</span>
>
> *What?! (Training started ten minutes ago!)*

*(Yusuf dashes off to the left. Aisha is flustered.)*

## Scene 5 · Home, 8:00 p.m. <span dir="rtl">(٨:٠٠ مَسَاءً)</span>

*On stage: Maryam. The wall clock shows 8:00 p.m.*

*(Yusuf walks in from the right.)*

**MARYAM** *(welcoming)* · `s5-01`

> <span dir="rtl">أَهْلًا يَا يُوسُفُ! كَمِ السَّاعَةُ؟</span>
>
> *Hi Yusuf! What time is it?*

*(Yusuf is flustered.)*

**YUSUF** *(exhausted, sighing)* · `s5-02`

> <span dir="rtl">الثَّامِنَةُ مَسَاءً... يَوْمٌ طَوِيلٌ!</span>
>
> *Eight in the evening... what a long day!*

**MARYAM** *(teasing, cheerful)* · `s5-03`

> <span dir="rtl">وَغَدًا يَبْدَأُ الدَّوَامُ فِي السَّابِعَةِ وَالنِّصْفِ!</span>
>
> *And tomorrow school starts at half past seven!*

*(Yusuf is shocked.)*

**YUSUF** *(disbelief, then he faints)* · `s5-04`

> <span dir="rtl">مَاذَا؟!</span>
>
> *What?!*

*(Yusuf faints! Maryam is flustered.)*

### ❓ Quiz 1 · What time is it?

*Noor stands by the chalkboard. A clock is drawn on the board.*

**NOOR** *(teacherly, inviting)* · `q-1`

> <span dir="rtl">كَمِ السَّاعَةُ؟</span>
>
> *Yusuf got this one wrong. What time is it?*

Options on the board: أ) <span dir="rtl">السَّابِعَةُ إِلَّا الرُّبْعَ</span> · ب) <span dir="rtl">الثَّامِنَةُ إِلَّا الرُّبْعَ</span> ✅ · ج) <span dir="rtl">السَّابِعَةُ وَالرُّبْعُ</span>

*(4-second countdown, then the answer is revealed.)*

### ❓ Quiz 2 · How long does the break last?

*Noor stands by the chalkboard.*

**NOOR** *(teacherly, inviting)* · `q-2`

> <span dir="rtl">كَمْ تَسْتَمِرُّ الاِسْتِرَاحَةُ؟</span>
>
> *How long does the break last?*

Options on the board: أ) <span dir="rtl">عِشْرِينَ دَقِيقَةً</span> ✅ · ب) <span dir="rtl">سَاعَةً</span> · ج) <span dir="rtl">خَمْسَ دَقَائِقَ</span>

*(4-second countdown, then the answer is revealed.)*

### ❓ Quiz 3 · What time is it?

*Noor stands by the chalkboard. A clock is drawn on the board.*

**NOOR** *(teacherly, inviting)* · `q-3`

> <span dir="rtl">كَمِ السَّاعَةُ؟</span>
>
> *And at the café: what time is it?*

Options on the board: أ) <span dir="rtl">الخَامِسَةُ وَخَمْسُ دَقَائِقَ</span> · ب) <span dir="rtl">الخَامِسَةُ إِلَّا خَمْسَ دَقَائِقَ</span> · ج) <span dir="rtl">السَّادِسَةُ إِلَّا خَمْسَ دَقَائِقَ</span> ✅

*(4-second countdown, then the answer is revealed.)*

### The end

*The whole cast lines up and hops. On screen: <span dir="rtl">النِّهَايَةُ</span>, the next lesson, and <span dir="rtl">بِالْعِلْمِ نَفْتَحُ الْأَبْوَابَ</span>.*

**NOOR** *(proud, happy)* · `end-1`

> <span dir="rtl">أَحْسَنْتُمْ!</span>
>
> *Well done, everyone!*
